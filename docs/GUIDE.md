# How to play Searcher

Searcher doesn't hold your hand. Your commander gives you one order, to find the enemy base, and then
leaves you with a ship that has too many systems, a bank that will ruin you, and a universe that wants
you dead. This guide covers what you can do, not only how to win.

Press **H** in the game at any time for the keys.

## Your first minute

Something is already hunting you, and it arrives about 9 seconds after you start.

1. Press **O** right away to dock. You start next to the ring. Menus pause the game, so you can take your time from here.
2. Press **I** for the station menu. You have $200,000.
3. Under **Upgrades**, buy **Better ship** at least four times. This raises your maximum frequency (see below).
4. Under **Auto systems**, try **Auto frequency**. It makes aiming and shield tuning automatic. Or do it by hand, which is harder and more interesting.
5. Wait for your energy to fill, then press **O** to leave.

## Flying

- **There is no friction.** Each press of a thrust key adds speed in that direction, and you keep going until you change it. **S** or **Space** stops you dead, because this ship is built on honesty rather than physics.
- **L** and **K** set your speed limit. The ceiling depends on your ship level.
- **The ship changes shape** with speed: a cross at rest, three thin ovals at full tilt.
- **]** is the autopilot: give it coordinates and a speed. Your position is always on the display.
- **T** is the hyperdrive. It jumps you a short way when fully charged, costs a quarter of your max energy, and can also move a rocket that is already in flight.
- **1** shows your home base and stops your ship while you look. **2** comes back.

## Energy is the real enemy

Everything burns energy, including standing still. Run out and you die, and the enemy base is further
away than a full tank will take you. Your options:

- **Dock (O)**: refills energy, shields, rockets (up to 10), tanks (up to 5), extra shields (up to 2) and repairs damage. You must be stopped, near the ring, with no enemy locked on. Docking draws on the base's own energy.
- **Energy tanks ([)**: +300 energy. Be careful: when your shield drops below 76, the ship burns a tank by itself to add 100 shield.
- **Solar panels (9)**: open them anywhere for fast charging. But your speed limit falls to 1, your shield is capped at 45, your lasers stop charging and your hyperdrive charge is wiped. Close them with 9 and the ship ramps back up to top speed.
  - With a full tank, open panels slowly make rockets and tanks and raise your maximums.
  - Opening them also raises a spare extra shield automatically and slowly builds new ones.
- **The tether**: within 5000 units of home, the base feeds you when you get below 50 energy.
- **Upgrade your ship**: a higher ship level means engines use less energy.

## Frequencies

This is the heart of combat, and the bit that makes Searcher odd.

- **Your laser only hits if its frequency exactly matches the target's.** Most enemies change frequency constantly. Miss, and you waste the shot. **N** and **M** tune your laser.
- **Enemy guns can only hurt you if they can reach your shield frequency.** So tune your shield (**V**, **B**) *above* the highest frequency they can reach. A hunter tops out at 11 to begin with. Your own maximum is `ship level x 2 + 2`, which is why upgrades come first.
- **Enemies catch up.** Every kill raises what that enemy can reach.
- **Auto frequency** does all this for you, and always hits. It's generous. Playing without it is a different game.

The cruiser's heavy shot ignores your frequency completely.

## Weapons and defences

- **J** switches lasers on, and they keep firing until you switch them off. They hit anything you have locked, however it moves.
- **P** arms the rocket launcher, which keeps launching at your locked target. You can't launch while going faster than 10.
- **The blast hurts you too.** Fire at something close without an extra shield up and you take the explosion. Your base can also be caught in it.
- **U** raises the extra shield. It keeps your normal shield topped up while it holds, costing energy, and it wears down under fire. You can't dock with it up.
- **Tab** locks onto the next enemy in range. **Y** clears the lock. Without a manual lock, the targeting always grabs the nearest attacker, so **you have to Tab onto the enemy base to shoot it**.

### Aimed rockets

Press **P** with nothing locked and you get to aim by hand: coordinates, fuse, and explosive power.

- **Power** sets the blast size and energy cost. There's no upper limit on what you can type.
- **Time bomb** set to 1 leaves the rocket sitting where you are until the fuse ends. Mines!
- **Yes, you can destroy the moon.** Yes, you can destroy Earth. Each takes a big enough blast nearby. The moon's end also stops the miner's deliveries to your base.
- You can also blow up your own home base, which ends the game.

### Tractor beam

Buy it for $3,000, lock a target and press **F** to choose which way to drag it. It only holds while your
beam frequency is at or above the target's. Drag fighters off you, or drag things into other things.

## The neighbourhood

- **Home base**, at 0,0. A docking ring on its right, and a refit bay beyond that. Its turrets defend it from raiders. Lose it and the game is over.
- **The miner**, the little yellow ship shuttling between the moon and home, keeps the base supplied.
- **Crates**, the glowing yellow boxes, appear within 5000 units of home. Worth $200 times the day of the month, so timing your pickups pays.
- **The moon**, at -5000, 6000. Getting close wrecks your shields.
- **Earth**, at 10000, -2000. **Do not go there.** Inside the dashed circle its gravity takes over your ship entirely. Getting out takes a hyperjump you probably can't make, or destroying the planet.
- **The force walls** box in the whole map, about 19000 units out. Touching one without an extra shield up is instant death. With one up, you pass through.
- **The gate**: the left wall has a gap at its centre that opens and closes. Good luck timing it.
- **Beyond the walls** there's empty space. And not only empty space.

## The enemies

| Enemy | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Hunter** | Hunts you from the start, and it's faster than you. Fires often, and locks onto your shield frequency if it can reach it. |
| **Raider** | Goes for your home base, not you, unless you get in its view. Your base turrets usually handle it, and you get paid for it. |
| **Cruiser** | Starts far away and arrives after about two and a half minutes, then never stops following. Big guns, and it runs for repairs when hurt. |
| **Enemy base** | Hidden. Weak guns, but many of them, plus the fact that everything respawns there. |

Destroyed enemies come back from the enemy base, stronger each time, up to level 10. Destroy the base and
it's rebuilt somewhere random, with bigger shields, and everything starts hunting you from there. The game
has no ending. How long you last is the score.

## Getting better

- **Upgrades** in the station menu are bought one step at a time.
- **Refit**: park inside the refit bay (you start there), then buy cycles at $1000 each. Each cycle raises ship, laser, rocket and extra shield levels at once, plus your maximums. It's much better value than single upgrades, but it stops if you move, get locked onto, or raise a shield.
- **Equipment**: rockets, tanks, extra shields, tractor beam, **Multicom** (open the station menu from anywhere) and **Tracer** (shows enemies on your map, with arrows on screen).
- **Auto systems** are free: lasers, rockets, frequency, refuelling and repair. Auto repair burns energy fast.

## The bank

Dock, press **I**, and enter the most dangerous place in the game.

- **Loans**, up to $20,000. Interest is 5% a *day*, and a day lasts under a second. Can't pay? The collectors shoot your shields, and that can kill you.
- **Savings accounts** pay out every month, and a month lasts about 25 seconds. The best account pays 30% each time. Do the maths. You get one account, and you can't open one while you have a loan.
- **Your PIN** appears once, when you open the account. Three wrong tries and the bank keeps everything. Write it down, like an adult.
- At the bank, typing **123123** as an amount means "all of it".

## Stuck?

- **"Cannot dock: danger nearby"**: you still have a target locked. Press **Y**, or deal with it.
- **"Too far from the docking ring"**: get closer to the ring, and come to a dead stop.
- **Lasers fire but nothing happens**: wrong frequency. Check the target panel, it says MATCH when you've got it.
- **Rockets won't launch**: slow down to 10 or under.
- **The game saves by itself.** Choose Continue on the title screen. Death wipes the save, as it should.
